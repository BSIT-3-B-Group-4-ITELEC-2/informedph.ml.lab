Title:  EXPLAINER: ICC Rules 68.2 and 68.3: How prior witness testimonies may be used in Duterte trial
Summary:  Rules 68.2 and 68.3 of the International Criminal Court's Rules of Procedure and Evidence were 
discussed during the final status conference for former president Rodrigo Duterte, whose trial begins on 
November 30. The main difference between the two rules is whether the witness must appear before the Trial 
Chamber. Rule 68.2 allows previously recorded testimony to be used even if the witness is not present under 
specific conditions, while Rule 68.3 requires the witness to be present in court and offer no objections. Legal experts note 
that these rules can help shorten court proceedings, though the defense has raised concerns about questioning witnesses 
whose evidence is crucial to the prosecution.
